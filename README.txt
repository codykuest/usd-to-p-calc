ISLAND CURRENCY - OFFLINE IPHONE WEB APP

Convert USD to:
- Bahamian Dollar (BSD), fixed at 1 USD = 1 BSD
- Jamaican Dollar (JMD), editable rate
- Mexican Peso (MXN), editable rate

INSTALL ON IPHONE:
1. Upload all files and folders in this project to a GitHub repository.
2. In the repository, enable Settings > Pages and deploy from the main branch/root.
3. Open the published HTTPS Pages URL in Safari on your iPhone while online.
4. Wait for the page to load, then tap Share > Add to Home Screen.
5. Launch Island Currency from the Home Screen. After the first successful online load, it works offline.

RATES:
- BSD is calculated at 1 USD = 1 BSD.
- JMD starts at 158.4342 JMD per USD.
- MXN starts at 17.5000 MXN per USD.
- These are editable starting reference values, not guaranteed live quotes.
- Tap Save rate to store your JMD and MXN rates on the iPhone.
- Offline mode cannot fetch live rates. Check your bank/card/exchange provider's rate and fees.
